Clear Pricing

Straight Answers and Upfront Prices

Good water heater service starts with knowing exactly what you're dealing with. Ellicott City Water Heater provides honest, upfront pricing on every job, handling everything from a burned-out heating element to a complete tank replacement. Every visit begins with a careful inspection of your water heater, including its age, capacity, fuel type, venting, and the condition of the tank and valves, followed by a clear recommendation and a written price before any work starts.

Many water heater problems stay hidden until a failure is well underway. Sediment settles on the bottom of the tank, the anode rod quietly dissolves, and the steel underneath starts to rust from the inside with no visible warning. If you notice rusty hot water, rumbling or popping sounds, a rotten-egg smell, hot water that runs out sooner than it used to, or moisture around the base, schedule an inspection sooner rather than later. An early diagnosis usually keeps a fixable issue from turning into a flooded floor.

We also help homeowners plan ahead. Most tank water heaters last roughly 8 to 12 years, and a planned water heater replacement is always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency swap with water on the floor. Our technicians know how hard water and local conditions affect tank life, so we can tell you honestly whether your heater has years left or is living on borrowed time.

Preventive Care

Tank Flushing and Tune-Ups That Pay Off

A maintenance visit from Ellicott City Water Heater is far more than opening the drain valve. Our technician flushes the sediment, pulls and inspects the anode rod, lifts and tests the relief valve, checks the burner, pilot, and venting on gas heaters, tests the elements and thermostats on electric units, and confirms a safe water temperature. Small problems get caught while they're still cheap to fix, and the heater stays running clean and efficient.

No two maintenance plans look exactly alike. An older tank needs a closer look than a new heater, a tankless unit on hard water calls for annual descaling to protect its heat exchanger working, and a large family works the heater far harder than a couple does. We set up each visit around your equipment and water, so every service keeps the unit efficient and protects the equipment you've already paid for.

The payoff shows up quickly. A tank without a sediment crust transfers heat more efficiently, a fresh anode rod can add years to the tank, and a tested relief valve is ready to do its job if pressure ever climbs too high. Compared with the cost of replacing a tank early, a maintenance plan is money well spent.

Proper Sizing

The Right Size Water Heater for Your Home

Proper sizing matters more than most homeowners realize. Before quoting a new heater, Ellicott City Water Heater looks at the details that determine your household's peak demand: the number of people and bathrooms, showers that run back to back, when the laundry and dishwasher run, and how cold the water entering the house gets. That gives you a heater with enough first-hour capacity or GPM to cover your busiest hour without paying to heat water you'll never use.

Picking a new water heater is about more than tank size. We'll compare tank models with tankless units, and gas with electric or heat pump, walking through operating costs, venting and gas line work, electrical service, and the installation itself. Tankless is a strong fit for many families, but it often needs a larger gas line and new venting, while a properly sized tank is often the simpler, lower-cost choice. The decision is yours, based on your home and budget.

Repairs We Handle

Common Water Heater Problems We Fix in Ellicott City

The majority of service calls we get come down to a handful of familiar problems. There's no hot water at all. The gas burner won't light, or it lights and shuts off. The shower goes cold after a few minutes, the tank pops every time the burner fires, or a tankless unit starts flashing an error code. Rusty water, a rotten-egg smell, and a dripping relief valve round out the list. Our technicians handle all of them, always targeting the component that actually failed.

Some failures can wait a day, and some can't. A failed element just means lukewarm showers. A relief valve spitting water, on the other hand, is a safety device doing its job and telling you something is wrong, and a slow leak at the base can quietly ruin subfloors and drywall before anyone notices. If you smell gas, get everyone outside and call the gas utility first. Our technicians carry the parts to replace elements, thermostats, thermocouples, gas valves, and relief valves, install expansion tanks where pressure is the problem, and handle leak repair at the drain valve or fittings.
