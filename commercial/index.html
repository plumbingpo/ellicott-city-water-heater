Restaurant Services

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Restaurants and food-service businesses have hot water demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Dish machines, three-compartment sinks, prep sinks, mop sinks, and handwashing stations all draw at once during a busy dinner service, and a tank with too little recovery runs cold just as the dish room fills up. Ellicott City Water Heater specializes in food-service hot water, including high-recovery gas tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters for high-temperature dish machines, correct sizing based on peak-hour demand, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Hot water temperature is a critical issue for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature can stop service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and flow your operation depends on, with written service records that show a well-maintained facility to health inspectors and landlords. We provide detailed reports after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

Replacements and maintenance visits are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we install overnight or before opening, so the kitchen opens the next day with hot water ready.

Code & Inspections

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide from a blocked or undersized vent, scalding at the tap, bacterial growth in tanks kept too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Ellicott City Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve and discharge line routing, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where local code calls for it, and the permit and inspection process. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and health or building inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms.
